# Review: cartoon-fx 0.10.3, its art and what it does

The audit behind round 3's intake, 2026-11-14. The owner asked to "review the fx plugin maybe you
can improve its functionality and/or its art". This file is evidence, not scope. It changes no
code. The owner chose which groups of work the round takes (`FEATURE-fun.md`); §6 says where
each finding landed. The repository's half of the same review is the root's
`docs/spec/REVIEW-2026-11-14.md`.

**Method.**
- **Read in full:** `hooks/register.tsx`, `hooks/fx.ts`, `hooks/detect.ts`, `types/index.d.ts`,
  the tests and the docs.
- **Drawn:** each of the ten drawings from `drawFx`, with `/fx`'s sample words, in a browser.
  Each was paused at 0.3, 0.55, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.95, 1.1, 1.2, 1.5, 1.6 and 1.9 s, on a dark
  and a light background, at 228, 300 and 570 px wide. The desktop draws the same SVG in a
  sandboxed frame (`isInteractive`), so this is the drawing; where the desktop puts it is not.
- **Probed:** `pushedBranch`, `committed` and `testsPassed` were called with the command and
  output of realistic runs (§7).

**Severity.** *High*: a promise the mod makes is broken. *Medium*: a fault the owner sees, or a
gap that will cost a later session. *Low*: drift. *Info*: recorded, no remedy.

## 2. What holds

- **No effect costs a call its result.** Every gating hook awaits `next(e)` first and has a
  `.catch` (`hooks/register.tsx:267-356`).
- **A failure plays nothing**, as tested: denied and errored calls, a failing run, a plan turned
  down.
- **The drawings are deterministic and script-free:** a seeded PRNG (`hooks/fx.ts:38-47`) and
  SMIL only.
- **Pure where it can be:** `fx.ts` and `detect.ts` touch no `$`.
- **The art has a style of its own:** rounded heavy type with ink outlines, squash and stretch,
  smoke puffs, motion rays. The stamp, the clapperboard, the swatter and the gauges read at a
  glance.

## 3. Findings

### High

**F1. One effect erases another before it is seen.** `play` replaces whatever is on show
(`hooks/register.tsx:154-164`). The context check runs beside every call's result (`:317`), so a
push that also crosses 75% has its confetti replaced by "Yellow zone!" at once. A real event
during `/fx all` is overwritten by the next sample (`:385-394`). One command that tests, commits
and pushes plays only the push (`:273-286`).

### Medium

**F2. The review's magnifying glass misses the page.** Its group is translated to
`(CX - 120, top + 10)` and its `animateMotion` values are absolute coordinates too
(`hooks/fx.ts:509-523`), so the offset counts twice. The lens sweeps below and right of the
page, half out of frame, and never passes over it. Seen at 0.45, 0.8 and 1.1 s.

**F3. The review's "!" marks overlap.** They sit beside lines 1, 3, 4 and 2 in that order
(`hooks/fx.ts:527`), 13 units apart with a radius of 8, so from three findings the second and
third overlap. Seen at 1.1 and 1.6 s.

**F4. The helpers hide behind their own smoke.** The smoke is drawn after the ninjas
(`hooks/fx.ts:458-475`) and covers them while they dash; at 39 units across, they are the
smallest figures of any effect. Seen at 0.95 and 1.2 s.

**F5. The gauge's bands disagree with the zones.** Yellow is drawn from 60 to 90 and red from 90
(`hooks/fx.ts:748-750`); the zones are 75 and 90 (`hooks/register.tsx:16-18`). At 76% the needle
sits well inside a yellow that started 16 points earlier.

**F6. Long words clip at the frame.** `label` pops to 1.28 times its size (`hooks/fx.ts:79`), and
"Bugs squashed!" at size 26 then runs past both sides of the 260-unit view. Its subtitle at
`y: 164` (`:651`) is cut by the bottom edge. Seen at 1.1 and 1.6 s.

**F7. The smoke goes muddy on the dark background.** Each puff is two circle layers, the
`#F4A261` edge under the `#FFF6E8` fill (`hooks/fx.ts:115-145`), and each circle fades on its
own, so the edge shows through the fading fill as brown discs. Seen in push, commit, compact and
fixes.

**F8. The push and the commit are read from loose patterns.**
- `gitPart` takes any part with `git` and later the word `push` or `commit`
  (`hooks/detect.ts:18`): `git log --grep=push` and a `# … push` comment count.
- With no `To <remote>` line, the branch is the first `x -> y` anywhere (`:59-61`): a log's
  decoration or a rename in a commit summary.
- A rejected push behind `; echo done` prints `main -> main` and plays.

§7 has each case.

**F9. Real commits and runs play nothing.**
- A commit on a detached HEAD prints `[detached HEAD sha]`, which the summary pattern
  (`hooks/detect.ts:71`) does not read.
- `node --test` (this repository's own tooling tests) is not a runner (`:29-34`), and its
  output, `pass 48` with the number after the word, would not read as a pass either.
- `rspec`, `dotnet test`, `gradle test` and `xcodebuild test` are not runners at all
  (`:29-34`).
- A passing run whose test names say "FAIL" is read as failing (`FAILED_MARK`, `:40`).

**F10. The engine reports moments that play nothing.** The Bash result's `gitOperation` names a
cherry-pick (`commit.kind`), a branch merged or rebased (`branch.action`) and a pull request
created, merged and more (`pr.action`) (the declarations' `claude-code-tools`, at 2.1.310). Today
only `push.branch` and `commit` are read.

**F11. The effects are big.** Every drawing is 832 × 608 px (`hooks/fx.ts:14`, `:827`). Under a
row in the desktop it pushes the conversation down by most of a screen, and nothing turns the
effects off.

**F17. A collapsed strip swallows effects.** From CLI 2.1.325 the person can collapse the strip
above the prompt, and `AbovePrompt`'s props say so (`isCollapsed`: "a tree still returns, and
nothing of it is on screen"). The strip's hook ignores it and marks the effect's row as drawn
(`hooks/register.tsx:478-481`). While it is collapsed, an effect the fallback hands to the strip,
and every effect under `/fx place bottom`, plays where nobody sees it. Which surfaces let the
strip collapse was not measured. Found when the gate went red on
the new prop; the test fixture was given `isCollapsed: false`, test-only, with no change of
behaviour.

### Low

**F12. Two effects in one millisecond share a seed,** and the seed is their identity: the
drawing cache (`hooks/register.tsx:55-62`), the end timer (`:160`) and the fallback (`:172`) key
by it.

**F13. Values past the drawn range are untested.** `drawFx` with more than five helpers, more
than four findings, or a percentage outside 0–100 has no test; the code clamps each.

**F14. Drift in comments.** A test title says "drawn twice the 0.5 size" where the scale is 3.2
(`tests/cartoon-fx.test.tsx:573`); `types/index.d.ts:10` lists the row kinds without `reply:`.

### Info

**F15. Nothing can fail when a drawing is wrong.** The tests check an `Svg` exists, its `alt`,
and its size string. F2–F7 passed every check. Rendering is outside what `claude plugin test` can
see; structure (positions, sizes, bands) is not.

**F16. The terminal shows one still row.** Five glyphs a side, coloured from the seed, for the
whole effect (`hooks/register.tsx:484-503`).

## 4. Duplication and drift

F14. The thresholds of F5 live in `register.tsx` while the bands live in `fx.ts`; one home would
have kept them together.

## 5. What round 3's work will put through the code

- **A queue** changes `play` and the `shown` contract: what waits, and what identifies an effect
  (F1, F12). Rows, the strip and the fallback all read `shown`.
- **New triggers** add kinds to `FxKind`, `FX_MS`, `drawFx`'s dispatch and `/fx`'s `SAMPLES`;
  `drawFx` is a nested ternary (`hooks/fx.ts:805-825`) that each one lengthens.
- **Controls** add `$.store` keys, which are machine identifiers.
